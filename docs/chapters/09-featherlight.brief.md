## What the chapter is

Module-1's chapter 9: **Sprinkle has to fly by the full moon. Thymeleaf makes a remedy for her wing,
and she gathers one ingredient from each territory: reed root from ReedClan, oak moss from GroveClan,
river mint from the Holt, dew from the glass towers. Then she lays out her case file for the
Gathering.** Gathering the oak moss finds the last dragonet (optional). Sprinkle practises flying. The
Count is the 8s (a spider's legs: double the 4s). Chapter 10 is the Gathering, at the full moon.

This is the warm chapter before the big one: the thief is known, the case is ready, and the job now
is to mend a friend. It should feel like a quest with friends from every corner of the river, a
little funny, and full of hope.

## The lesson kit (the Counts, Aron's first priority)

- **The picture: a spider's legs.** A tiny garden spider spinning in a corner of Thymeleaf's den:
  eight legs, **four on each side**. "An eight is two fours." Friendly, small, never creepy: she waves.
- **A skip-count Tap:** spiders' legs, 8, 16, 24, 32, 40.
- **Ten facts on the 8s, both orders mixed. She already knows almost all the eights:** 8 × 1, 2 × 8,

  8 × 8.** All three are in the ten, twice if a retry brings one back; the rest are stepping stones
  ("Four sevens is twenty-eight. Double it: fifty-six."). Leave one or two facts without a right line

- **Story questions (two or three): remedy doses.** Thymeleaf's remedy is measured out in eights
  (eight drops, eight leaves, eight days): e.g. "Eight drops, three times a day" (3 × 8), or the days

- **Mizzle's riddle** (optional) is a fact not yet a riddle (7 × 5, 4 × 10, 7 × 1, 7 × 3, 4 × 4, and
  Sprinkle's 2 × 5 and 6 × 5 are taken); an 8s fact is best.
- Help after a miss is the sand, and the fact comes back two questions later. Never a wall; no clock.
- At bedtime the ninth claw mark is on the tree.

## Steering calls (Claude's, under decision 46)

1. **The days.** The moon is eight evenings old; the Gathering is at the full moon, about evening 14.
   **This chapter covers five days, days 9 to 13**, and ends on the eve of the Gathering (the moon
   nearly round). Day 9 is told in full (the morning, the lesson, Thymeleaf, the first ingredients);
   the rest is a quick, warm montage of days (the gathering finished, the remedy, Sprinkle's practice),
   then evening 13 in full (the case laid out, Glintstar). Chapter 10 opens on the day of the Gathering.
2. **The thefts have stopped.** Tallyheart knows the tunnel on both paths now. She sits by its mouth
   at night, and the next morning the pile is whole: "Forty. All forty. Not one piece gone." Said, not
   asked. It stays whole to the Gathering. Nobody blocks the tunnel: it is evidence.
3. **The kept path tells Tallyheart about Sprinkle**, early on day 9: Sprinkle needs a medicine cat's
   remedy, and that is a grown-up's help. Her secret has got too big to carry alone ("Your secret is
   getting bigger."), and Tallyheart's "I'm right here" from chapter 3 pays off. Tallyheart is not
   cross: "I knew there was something under that moss." From here both paths know the same things.
   The told path has its own short morning beat (Riffle hears every bit of the trail).
4. **Thymeleaf**, CrystalClan's medicine cat (module-1's, on screen for the first time), **a tom**: old
   and calm, twinkly-eyed, smelling of mint, who talks to his herbs. His den is a nook of the camp full
   of drying leaves. He is told about Sprinkle (by Tallyheart) and says yes at once: "A wing is a wing."
   He names the four ingredients and why each: reed root (it holds), oak moss (it soothes), river mint
   (it cools), and dew from the glass towers (the first light of the morning, gathered high up).
5. **The four ingredients, quickly and warmly**, one from each corner of the river:
   - *Reed root, from ReedClan*: Sloshbank brings it to the wall's end at the border ("ReedClan will
     think about it." paid off: they thought). No CrystalClan paw on ReedClan's mud.
   - *Oak moss, from GroveClan*: her one new place. GroveClan is the oldest Clan; its cats remember
     everything and say so. She and her friend cross the little bridge over the channel to the grove
     (no raft: the otters' ferry raft is chapter 10's Moon Ferry), and a GroveClan elder lets her take
     moss from the old oak. That is where Mizzle can be found (call 6).
   - *River mint, from the Holt*: Riffle brings it from home ("Granny says it's the BEST mint"). The
     Holt is named; nobody goes there.
   - *Dew from the glass towers*: at dawn, high up. With Waffles' help (the little room that goes DING)
     to the roof, where dew lies on the rain puddle by the red light. **A cat who never met Squall meets
     him here**: chapter 4's roof scene, offered once more (promised in chapter 5; `ch9Roof`). A cat who
     knows him gets one loud hello ("I'm still GUARDING!"). Squall's "five" and Hail's reach Sprinkle.
6. **Mizzle (optional), the last dragonet:** in a hollow in the old oak, pearly green, sweet and
   sleepy, always about to nod off. She knows which moss is the right moss for a dragon's wing (the
   soft silver kind on the north side), and that is why she matters. A riddle (call it a sleepy
   question), her "five" for Sprinkle, `foundMizzle`, her card (gated): what she saw on the storm night,
   or what she knows; her page. Saying no sets nothing; the moss is still found (the elder shows it).
   Module-1 called her Dew: **she is Mizzle** (call 11).
7. **The remedy and the flying.** Thymeleaf mixes it (the doses are the story's Counts). Days of
   rubbing it in, and Sprinkle practises: a hop, a flap, a wobble, a short glide off the bank, a
   splash into the shallows (bright, sunny water, never dark), and by evening 13 a real lift: a few
   wingbeats in the air. **She is not fully mended yet**: whether her wing holds is the Gathering's.
   Joyful and funny, never a fall that hurts.
8. **Laying out the case for the Gathering**, evening 13: in her nest she lifts the moss and lays out
   her bark, row by row, with Tallyheart: the moon, FLEW, the piles and the stash, the scale and the
   glass, Coil's diggers and the tunnel. Read the rows in a sentence each. "That's not a story. That's
   a case." Nothing is "answered" yet: picking the cards to answer Reynard is chapter 10's.
9. **Glintstar is told, evening 13, on both paths:** Tallyheart keeps her chapter 4 promise and tells
   her everything, Sprinkle included. Glintstar is fair and careful: a dragonet who cannot fly did not
   steal the prey, and a fox who lies at her border will be answered at the Gathering, "by the one who
   found it out." She does not tell the Clan yet. The apprentice will speak at the Gathering (her talk
   worry's moment is chapter 10's; one line here at most).
10. **Riffle** is everywhere: the mint, the jokes, carrying Hail's "five". Coil may get one line
    (Sprinkle's practice is NOISY; he asked for quiet). Waffles is delighted to be useful.
11. **Names.** Dew is **Mizzle** (an exact Warriors name otherwise; Mizzle checked clear on both
    wikis). Thymeleaf, GroveClan and the Holt checked clear. GroveClan's cats stay unnamed (labels:
    "A GroveClan elder", "A GroveClan apprentice").
12. **The warrior thread:** she has gathered from every territory and spoken to every Clan; Glintstar
    says she will speak at the Gathering. One warm moment; no promise of a name.
13. **The hook** for chapter 10 (*The Gathering*): tomorrow, the full moon, the Moon Ferry, every Clan,
    and Reynard. Curious and brave, never scary.
