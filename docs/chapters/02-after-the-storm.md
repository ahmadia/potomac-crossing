# Chapter 2: After the Storm

*Draft v0.5 · 2026-10-04 · about 15–20 minutes to play*

*How to read this draft: each line (---) is a new screen, about one comic panel. **Choose** marks a
choice, **Counts** marks a Counts moment (she types the answers on the keypad), and **Tap** marks
something she taps. `{name}` is the first part of her Clan name (it makes {name}paw) and
`{petname}` is what her Tall One calls her. It’s written for a she-cat; a tom reads he and him. A
line marked like *[if worry: water]* shows only to a player who made that choice in chapter 1, and
one marked *[if ch2Path: bridge]* only on that path. After “The way home” the story splits in two,
and the paths meet again at “Home.” Notes in italics are for us, not for her. Sound effects are in
italics too; they’re hers, and they go on screen.*

## The prey pile

Under an arch of brambles is the prey pile. There are mice and voles with their tails tucked in,
all stacked in pairs. It smells like breakfast.

---

*[if specialty: noticing]* Behind the pile, the earth looks freshly dug. Rain, probably.

---

“My patrol stacked that last night, before the storm. Ten pairs. I bet a pillow cat doesn’t even
know how many that is.”

*Then the real pile. Its stacks light one at a time as she noses them, with no number shown.*

## The wall above the river

## The Old Bridge

At the Old Bridge, the reeds along the bank are bent and broken.

“This is where the crash came from,” says Tallyheart.

---

“Best den on the whole river. And no cat goes under there. Not ever. Because…”

*CLANKETY-CLANK! RUMMMBLE-RUMMMBLE!*

other way.

Tallyheart’s mouth is still moving, but all you can hear is the train.

---

“…and THAT is the whole reason,” says Tallyheart, very pleased with herself.

You didn’t hear a word.

## Under the bridge

*If she peeks under the bridge.*

Riffle’s fur stands straight up. “Under THERE? Nope, nope, nope! I’ll keep watch!” He hops onto a
rock at the edge of the shadow.

---

You step into the dark. It’s cool in here, and dry further back.

---

Deep drag marks come up out of the river, through the mud, and on into the dark.

---

You follow them, one paw at a time. Then you hear it: a small, wet sniffle.

It doesn’t sound scary. It sounds sad.

---

“Hello?” you whisper.

At the very back, two big eyes open. Round as river stones, and shining.

---

Then they’re gone. There’s only the dark, and the *drip, drip, drip*.

---

You back out into the sunshine… and bump right into something big and soft and ginger.

---

“I looked down from the bank and counted one apprentice,” says Tallyheart. “Then I counted none.”

---

“I’m not angry. I was scared. That’s different.”

---

---

“That’ll be Coil,” says Tallyheart. “Nosy old thing.”

---

“Hm. Whatever’s under there, it isn’t for apprentices. Home.”

*Blink. Blink. Blink.*

“No,” you say.

---

---
