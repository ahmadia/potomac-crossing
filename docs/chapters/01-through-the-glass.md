# Chapter 1: Through the Glass

*Draft v0.1 · 2026-10-03 · about 15–20 minutes to play*

*How to read this draft: each line (---) is a new screen. **Choose** marks a choice and **Type**
marks something she types. `{name}` is the Clan name she picks ("Moon" makes Moonpaw) and
`{petname}` is what her Tall One calls her. It's written for a she-cat; a tom reads he and him.
Notes in italics are for us, not for her.*

## The window

Every evening you sit in the same spot: the warm cushion by the big glass door, where the
garden begins.

Outside, the glass towers glow orange in the sunset. Airplanes drift down over the river, one
after another, humming through the glass.

And in the garden, if you watch very carefully, the wild cats come out.

---

You've watched them for moons. A big ginger tabby who counts the sparrows out loud. A silver
leader who sits on the old stone fountain like a queen. Apprentices who pounce on leaves, and
miss.

You know every one of them. Not one of them knows you.

---

"PSSST. Down there! Pillow cat!"

The voice comes from far, far above. Nineteen floors up, a fluffy white face peers over a
balcony railing.

It's Princess Waffles. Princess Waffles has never touched grass, and she has never once stopped
talking.

---

"Today on *As the Garden Turns*," Waffles calls down, "the big ginger one counted FORTY-TWO
sparrows, the leader looked VERY impressed, and an apprentice fell in the birdbath. Thrilling. I
had to lie down."

She peers at you.

"You're doing the face again, darling. The wanting-to-go-outside face."

---

You catch your reflection in the glass. What does Waffles see?

**Choose:** she-cat or tom

**Choose your fur:** midnight black · snow white · silver tabby · brown tabby · ginger · cream ·
grey · tortoiseshell · calico

**Choose a marking (if you like):** white paws · a white chest · a dark stripe down your back ·
a splash of white on your nose

**Choose your eyes:** green · amber · blue · copper · one blue and one green

---

"And what does the Tall One call you these days?" Waffles asks. "Remind me."

**Choose or type your pet name:** Muffin · Snickerdoodle · Mittens · Biscuit · Sir Pounce-a-lot ·
*(type your own)*

"{petname}," Waffles sighs. "Adorable. Utterly un-wild."

---

**What did you do all day, as a pillow cat?**
- Watched the birds through the glass. *(noticing)*
- Hid in the laundry basket and ambushed socks. *(sneaking)*
- Climbed the curtains. The Tall One was NOT pleased. *(climbing)*
- Played in the dripping kitchen tap, the only cat in the towers who likes water. *(swimming)*
- Chatted with Princess Waffles for hours and hours. *(making friends)*

*Waffles answers each one. Birds: "You do have a stare. It's unsettling. I mean that as a
compliment." Socks: "Legendary. Nobody's socks are safe." Curtains: "I heard about the curtains.
The whole building heard about the curtains." Tap: "Water. On purpose. You are a mystery to me."
Chatting: "Obviously. The best use of anyone's time."*

## The open door

Behind you, the Tall One hums a little song and slides the glass door open to water her plants.
Cool evening air pours in. It smells of leaves, and river, and something wild.

A moth flutters past your nose, out the door, into the garden.

**Choose:**
- Chase the moth! *(You bound after it and land, all four paws at once, in…)*
- Step out slowly, one paw at a time. *(The doorframe slides past your whiskers, and you are
  standing in…)*

---

Grass.

You have never touched grass. It is prickly. It is cold. It is a little bit wet. It is the most
WONDERFUL thing you have ever felt.

From nineteen floors up comes a faint, faraway shriek of joy: "SHE'S DOING IT!"

---

Something makes you look back.

The Tall One stands in the doorway with her watering can. She watches you for a long moment.
Then she smiles a small smile, sets a dish of water on the warm step, and leaves the door open a
crack.

You don't know how Tall Ones know things. But she knows.

*This is the reassurance beat: her old home stays open, and she can visit it from camp.*

## Thirteen sparrows

The garden is bigger from the inside. The hedge is taller. The shadows are deeper.

On the fence, a row of sparrows is settling down for the night. Below them sits the big ginger
tabby, counting under her breath.

"…ten, eleven, twelve. Twelve sparrows."

---

You look again. There's one more, half hidden on top of the lamp post, fluffed up like a
dandelion.

**Choose:**
- "Thirteen! There's one on the lamp!"
- Stay very, very quiet.

*Speak up:* The ginger cat's ears swivel toward you. She looks at the lamp. She looks at you.
"Thirteen," she says slowly. "Sharp eyes."

*Stay quiet:* You don't make a sound. But the ginger cat's nose twitches. "Soap," she says,
without turning around. "And… tuna. Come out, little pillow cat. How many sparrows do you see?"
You tell her: thirteen. "Sharp eyes," she says.

---

Up close, she is enormous, with a torn ear and the kindest eyes you have ever seen.

"I'm Tallyheart," she says. "I count things. Pawsteps, sparrows, stars. My mother named me
Tallykit because I wouldn't stop counting my brothers and sisters." She tilts her head. "You've
been watching us through the glass. Every evening, for moons."

You didn't know she'd seen you.

"Counting cats notice things," says Tallyheart. "Come on. Let's see what Glintstar makes of you."

## Behind the hedge

Tallyheart slips through a gap in the hedge you never knew was there. You follow.

On the other side, the garden goes wild.

---

Brambles arch over secret dens. Ferns grow taller than you. Moss covers the old stone paths,
and on the dry fountain in the middle sits a silver cat, watching everything.

Glass towers rise all around, golden in the last of the light. But down here, hidden from every
Tall One in the world, is a whole Clan of cats.

CrystalClan.

---

Cats come out of the dens to stare. Whiskers twitch. Somebody sniffs.

"That's a pillow cat."

"She smells like soap."

"Look at those soft paws. She's never caught a thing in her life."

---

The silver cat on the fountain stands. This is Glintstar, leader of CrystalClan, and her eyes
are the color of the river in winter.

"Tallyheart," she says. "Why have you brought a pillow cat into our camp?"

"Because she saw a sparrow I missed," says Tallyheart.

A few cats laugh. Glintstar doesn't.

---

Glintstar looks down at you. "What is your name, pillow cat?"

"{petname}," you say.

There is a very long silence.

"{petname}," Glintstar repeats, with tremendous dignity.

Somewhere in the ferns, an apprentice snorts so hard he falls over.

---

"Why should CrystalClan take in a pillow cat?" asks Glintstar.

**Choose:**
- "I want to learn everything. Hunting, borders, all of it." *(Glintstar's whiskers twitch.
  "Everything is a great deal. But wanting to learn is a start.")*
- "I counted the sparrows. I can learn to count anything." *("Tallyheart's own gift," says
  Glintstar, glancing at the ginger cat. "Interesting.")*
- "I'm braver than I look." *("You would have to be," murmurs Glintstar. A few cats laugh, but
  not unkindly.)*

---

Tallyheart steps forward. "Let her try. I'll teach her myself."

"She was born behind glass," growls a grizzled old tom.

"So?" says Tallyheart. "Judge her by what she does, not by where she sleeps."

---

Glintstar is quiet for a long moment. Then she nods.

"One moon," she says. "Until the full moon, and the Gathering. Learn our ways. Keep the Accord.
Then we will see if you belong here."

One moon. You can do anything for one moon.

## Your Clan name

"Pillow names don't hunt," says Glintstar. "A cat who joins CrystalClan chooses a Clan name.
What shall we call you?"

**Type the first part of your Clan name.** *If she asks for ideas: Moon · Fern · Minnow · Frost ·
Pebble · Sedge · Storm · Holly.*

---

Glintstar's voice rings out over the garden.

"Cats of CrystalClan! From this night, this cat is {name}paw. Tallyheart will teach her to hunt,
to guard our borders, and to know her Counts. Welcome her."

---

One by one, the cats begin to purr. The sound spreads from den to den, low and warm, until the
whole wild garden is humming your new name.

{name}paw.

Far above, a tiny voice wails, "I'm CRYING, darling! I've coughed up a hairball!"

## The Counts

Tallyheart leads you to a sandy hollow at the edge of camp, under an old tree that leans over it
like it's listening.

"Every warrior learns two things first," she says. "The hunter's crouch, and her Counts."

"Counts?"

"The crouch feeds you. The Counts feed your whole Clan. A cat who knows her Counts can share the
prey pile fairly. She knows when a patrol comes home one cat short." Tallyheart's eyes glint.
"And she can always tell when something's missing. Remember that."

---

She points her tail at three cats dozing in the last patch of sun.

"We start with tails. Every cat has one tail. Even pillow cats. How many tails on those three?"

**Counts: the 1s (tails).** *A picture first (three sleeping cats, three tails), then she types.*

`3 × 1 = ?`

"Ha! Easy, yes? Again."

---

*The lesson runs 8–10 answers, all 1s, in both orders: 1 × 5, 6 × 1, 1 × 1, 9 × 1, 1 × 10, 4 × 1,
1 × 7, 8 × 1. Each starts with a picture: cats in a row, one tail each.*

*After a miss, Tallyheart scratches one line in the sand for each tail and counts them with her,
slowly: "There. We'll come back to that one." It comes back two questions later.*

*Tallyheart along the way:* "You didn't even have to count that time." · "Tails are easy. That's
why we start with them. Tomorrow: ears."

---

When you're done, Tallyheart stretches up and scratches a single claw mark into the leaning tree.

"That's your first Count," she says. "The tails. One day this tree will have a mark for every
Count you know, and they'll glow."

## The worry

Tallyheart walks you to the apprentices' den, a hollow under an enormous rosebush. Then she stops
and looks at you with her kind eyes.

"Everyone's scared of something on their first night," she says. "What's yours?"

**Choose:**
- "What if I'm too small?" *("Small cats fit where big cats can't. You'll see.")*
- "The river. It looks so deep." *("Then we'll take it one paw at a time.")*
- "When I'm nervous, I talk and talk and talk." *("Good. A Clan needs a cat who'll speak up.")*
- "Shiny things. I can't stop looking at them." *("Then you'll be the first to notice when
  something sparkles that shouldn't.")*

*Each worry becomes her brave moment later on; see Paths in `module-1.md`.*

## The Sky River

Before you go in, Tallyheart tips her head back. "Look up."

You've seen stars before, through the glass, a few at a time.

You have never seen this.

---

The sky is enormous. Across the whole of it runs a river of light: glittering, shimmering, a
thousand thousand stars.

"The Sky River," Tallyheart says softly. "The cats who came before us live there. They watch over
the Clans. And sometimes, if you're lucky, they send you a dream."

She starts to count the stars, then laughs. "Even I can't count those."

## The storm

The apprentices' den smells of moss and rose leaves. Two apprentices are already asleep: a
skinny grey tom who snores like the Tall One's vacuum cleaner, and a she-cat who mutters
"mice… mice… mice…" in her sleep.

You make a nest of moss, turn around three times, and curl up. It isn't a cushion. But it's yours.

---

Late in the night, the stars go out.

Wind shakes the rosebush. Rain drums on the leaves. Thunder booms so loud the ground shivers,
and you press yourself flat in your nest.

You have never heard a storm without glass between you and it.

---

"Storms pass." Tallyheart's voice comes from just outside, low and steady. She's lying across the
doorway in the rain, like a big ginger wall. "I'm right here."

---

Then, far off, toward the river: a CRASH.

Something huge. Something heavy. A splash like a whole tree falling into the water.

Every cat in camp lifts their head.

---

Tallyheart's torn ear twitches toward the river. Then she settles down again.

"Whatever that was," she says, "it'll still be there in the morning. Sleep, {name}paw."

And with the rain on the leaves and Tallyheart at the door, you do.
