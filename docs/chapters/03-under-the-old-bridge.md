# Chapter 3: Under the Old Bridge

*Draft v0.4 · 2026-10-06 · about 20 minutes to play*

*How to read this draft: each line (---) is a new screen, about one comic panel. **Choose** marks a
choice, **Counts** marks a Counts moment (she types the answers on the keypad), and **Tap** marks
something she taps. `{name}` is the first part of her Clan name (it makes {name}paw), `{petname}` is
what her Tall One calls her, and `{they}` becomes she or he. It’s written for a she-cat; a tom reads
he and him. A line marked like *[if specialty: noticing]* or *[if worry: shiny]* shows only to a
player who made that choice in chapter 1, one marked *[if ch2Path: bridge]* or *[if ch2Stone: nose]*
only to a player who did that in chapter 2, and one marked *[if ch3Told: true]* only on that path of
this chapter. After “Tell or keep” the story splits in two, and the paths meet again at “Home.” Notes
in italics are for us, not for her. Sound effects are in italics too; they’re hers, and they go on
screen.*

## Claws

**Tap** the paws, left to right. *The next paw to tap glows softly. A tap on it lights its five
claws, and a big number grows by five while Tallyheart says it: 5, 10, 15, 20. A tap anywhere else
just makes the glowing paw wiggle, so there’s nothing to get wrong. The fifth paw is the old tom’s,
and tapping it turns the page, where his claws are lit too and the number has reached 25.*

`4 × 5 = ?`
