# Chapter 10 brief: The Gathering

*v1 · 2026-10-24 · the orchestrator's brief for the text workflow (`docs/production.md`, "The plan
for chapters 6 to 10"). Every drafter, judge, critic and editor reads this. The calls below are
Claude's under decision 46, recorded in the README; Aron can reverse any of them. Decisions 48 to 51
are Aron's.*

## What the chapter is

Module-1's chapter 10, the finale: **on the Moon Ferry upriver, Tallyheart runs the Counts with her
to steady her nerves. On Gathering Island, Reynard makes his case, and she answers each claim with a
clue from her file (she picks which; a wrong pick gets a gentle nudge, never a fail). Snip and Snatch
start blaming each other and confess, and the foxes are chased off the territories. Then the mist
rolls in: the Mistscales have come for their clutch. Sprinkle's wing holds, and the dragonets she
found come to stand beside her before they fly (if that's all seven, the eldest Mistscale bows low to
her). Sprinkle presses the grey pebble into her paws, and under the full moon it flashes blue and
green: a mist stone, the Mistscales' thanks. Glintstar honours her in front of every Clan: her moon
is over, and she belongs to CrystalClan. She is still {name}paw, and Tallyheart tells her the warrior
name is coming, with this moon in it.** Then the Warrior Counts open (decisions 48 and 49). The hook
is a badger's track in the mud upriver.

This is the whole module coming home: every clue she gathered gets its moment, her worries get their
last brave moments (water on the Moon Ferry, talk at the Gathering), and every friend is there. It
should feel big, warm and earned, funny where it can be, and never frightening.

## The budget (decision 50: the finale may run to about 30 minutes)

- **The plain path at most about 2,700 words** (about 30 minutes) on any one reading.
- **One optional scene, Puddle's, at most about 300 words**: under the otters' ferry raft (the Moon
  Ferry), for a cat who never found him (`ch10Puddle`, as chapter 5's `ch5Roof` pattern).
- About **18 to 22 typed answers** (the lesson, the Moon Ferry Counts) and **five or six card picks**.
- At most **170 frames** in a reading.
- **New cast:** the grown Mistscales (Sprinkle's Mama and a few shapes in the mist; "Mama" is a
  title, as in chapter 3), ReedClan's and GroveClan's leaders (labels only: "ReedClan's leader",
  "GroveClan's leader"), the crowd of every Clan. Everyone else is drawn already.
- **New places:** Gathering Island (the clearing under the great tree, the full moon) and the Moon
  Ferry on the river at night (the otters' raft, moonlit, bright silver-blue, **never dark**). Two
  places, as module-1 forces: production plans this chapter as two build windows.
- **One new engine feature: the card pick.** Reynard makes a claim; she opens her Case File and picks
  the card that answers it; a wrong pick gets a gentle nudge ("Hmm. Does that one say anything about
  the MOON?") and she picks again; it can never fail. Everything else is what chapters 1 to 9 built.
- **Flags:** `ch10Puddle` (sticky), and at most one choice flag the Warrior Ceremony chapter reads.

## The lesson kit (the Counts, Aron's first priority)

- **Warm-up:** two questions on the 8s "to wake up your whiskers", one adapting to her hardest 8s
  fact (continuity's "What chapter 10 inherits" has the avoid list).
- **The picture (decision 51): a clutch of seven.** Seven dragonets in a clutch, Sprinkle's six
  brothers and sisters and her. "How many claws on seven forepaws?" was Squall's riddle (7 × 5).
- **She knows every seven but one.** 7 × 1 to 7 × 10 have all come up (chapters 1 to 9); **7 × 7 is
  the only new fact.** Ask it in the lesson with stepping stones ("Six sevens is forty-two. One more
  seven: forty-nine."), and let it come back once more later in the chapter. The lesson's ten facts
  are a mixed review of the sevens, 7 × 7 among them.
- **The Moon Ferry Counts** (module-1): on the raft upriver, Tallyheart runs the Counts with her to
  steady her nerves, **every table mixed**, Clanmates cheering from the raft. Help is always there,
  and it can't be failed. About eight questions, one or two from each of her ten Counts.
- Help after a miss is the sand (on the raft: a log, as chapter 5's), the fact comes back two
  questions later. Never a wall; no clock.
- At bedtime the tenth claw mark is on the tree: "One day, there'll be ten marks here." (chapter 3).

## Steering calls (Claude's, under decision 46)

1. **The day.** The full moon, day 14. Morning: a first screen with a friend-near line for a
   water-worry cat (she went to sleep on "Tomorrow: the otters' Moon Ferry."); the 8s warm-up and the
   7s. Dusk: the Moon Ferry upriver. Night: Gathering Island, the case, the confession, the mist, the
   Mistscales, the stone, Glintstar. Late: home, the tenth claw mark, her nest, the dream question,
   the hook.
2. **The Moon Ferry: water's brave moment.** The otters raft the Clans upriver to the island
   (world.md). A water-worry cat steps aboard at night, one paw at a time (chapter 5's words), and the
   river is moonlit silver-blue, bright, never dark (the README rule). Tallyheart runs the Counts with
   her to steady everyone's nerves. Puddle may be found under the raft (the optional scene); Riffle and
   Granny are aboard.
3. **Gathering Island.** Every Clan under the full moon, on the truce (the Accord): CrystalClan,
   ReedClan (Sloshbank, the she-cat, the young tom), GroveClan (the elder who remembers Tallyheart
   counting out loud), the leaders. Glintstar opens: the dragon's case is heard tonight. Reynard is
   there to make it (charming, vain, sure of himself), with Snip and Snatch behind him.
4. **The case, card by card: talk's brave moment.** Reynard makes his claims one at a time; she
   answers each by picking a card from her file, and says it out loud (a talk-worry cat: "You hold it
   in no longer."). **Every claim must be answerable by a card every player holds on every path**:
   - "It FLEW off with your prey" → `cant-fly` (Sprinkle can't fly; `mizzle-wing` too, if held).
   - "By the light of the moon" → `waffles-no-moon` or `heron-no-moon` (two witnesses: no moon).
   - "It left a dragon scale by your pile" → `glass` (it's tower glass) or `scale`.
   - "Who else could take so much?" → `prints-sprinkle` (the thief's prints are tiny) or `stash`.
   - "I never even SAW your pile" → `fox-brag` (his "little story", to get the dry den) or `tunnel`.
   Nothing may require `my-count`, `mizzle-wing` or any optional card (chapter 9's spec, section 8).
   A wrong pick gets a gentle nudge and another try; it can never fail.
5. **The confession.** Caught out, Snip and Snatch start blaming each other ("It was HER idea!" "It
   was the FOX's idea!") and then everything tumbles out. Funny, never cruel. The foxes are chased
   off the territories by every Clan standing together, no fighting, nobody hurt: they slink off into
   the dark, and somewhere the Barking Field dogs bark goodbye. ReedClan and CrystalClan get their
   prey back from the stash (a line).
6. **The mist and the Mistscales.** Mist rolls in over the island: the grown Mistscales have come for
   their clutch: huge, gentle, misty, never frightening (Sprinkle's Mama first). **Sprinkle's wing
   holds**: she flies, really flies, to her Mama. The dragonets she found come to stand beside her and
   say goodbye to her by name; the ones she never found are shapes in the mist ("to be met another
   time"). **All seven found:** the eldest Mistscale bows low to her (decision 20; a small extra, never
   needed). All seven get home either way (decision 19).
7. **The mist stone.** Sprinkle presses the plain grey pebble she has held since chapter 3 into her
   paws, and under the full moon it flashes blue and green: a mist stone (decision 18: labradorite,
   the real stone). Say what it is in the story's words only; the real stone is Aron's to give.
8. **Glintstar honours her** in front of every Clan: her moon is over, and she belongs to CrystalClan
   (chapter 1's "one moon"). She is still {name}paw: no warrior name (decision 13 as amended by 34).
   Tallyheart tells her the warrior name is coming, with this moon in it, **when she passes the Warrior
   Counts** (decision 48), and that she can take them whenever she is ready (the hub offers them after
   this chapter: `docs/warrior-counts.md`).
9. **Home.** Riffle, Waffles (from her balcony, of course), Coil (one quiet line), Squall's roar from
   the tower if he flew home with the clutch, Murmurchime, the old tom. The tenth claw mark. Her nest,
   the stone on the moss. The dream question.
10. **The hook** (module 2, not this module's): a badger's track in the mud upriver. Curious, never
    scary.
11. **Paths:** told and kept know the same since chapter 9. Read `specialty` and `worry` for a line
    each at their moments; the found flags for the goodbyes; `ch3Told` only where the past differs.

## Names (checked 2026-10-24, before drafting)

- **Words:** "the Accord", "the truce", "the Gathering", "a mist stone", "Mistscale". Not "StarClan",
  "Twoleg", "kittypet", "Ironsnake"; no "whisker-length" or "claw-length".

## Format

Chapter 9's story format exactly: the title "# Chapter 10: The Gathering", an italic header with the
minutes, "How to read this draft" (only the marks this chapter uses), `##` sections, `---` between
screens, **Choose:**, **Type**, **Tap**, **Pick a card** (the new interaction: write the claim, the
right cards, and the nudge for a wrong one), **New card**, `[if …]` variants, second person present,
the tokens, sound effects in italics. It ends warm in her nest on the dream question and the hook.

**The story only.** No notes beyond a closing block of at most 40 lines, `*For us*`, with the words
and minutes on every path, the new names used, the calls made (one line of reason each), and what the
Warrior Counts and the Ceremony chapter inherit in a few lines. The flags, Counts sets, cards, the card
pick's design, art needs and build notes go in the spec (`10-the-gathering.spec.md`).
