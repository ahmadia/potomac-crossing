# The world of Potomac Crossing

*v0.2 · 2026-10-03 · Our own world: none of these names come from the books.*

## In one breath

Along the Potomac, three Clans of cats hunt the parks and marshes, otters run the ferry that
ties the river together, and dragons live in the mist of the Great Falls gorge. An old
promise, the Accord, keeps them from hunting each other. Foxes, weasels, badgers, dogs and
snakes never made that promise.

## The Accord

- **Who keeps it:** the Clans (cats), the Holt (otters), the Mistscales (dragons), and the
  river's quiet neighbors: herons, crows, turtles, beavers. No one who keeps the Accord
  hunts anyone else who keeps it.
- **Who doesn't:** foxes, weasels, badgers, dogs and snakes. They talk, they scheme, and
  most of them are bad guys.
- **Who doesn't talk:** mice, voles, fish and the rest of the prey.
- **Tallwalkers** (humans) are everywhere and notice nothing. They never look up.

## Cats: the three Clans

- **CrystalClan:** climbers and sun-lovers, quick-witted, a little vain about their shiny
  territory. Leader: Glintstar. Every player's Clan in module one.
- **ReedClan:** patient marsh hunters, the only cats who will get their paws wet (a
  little). Proud, and suspicious of CrystalClan.
- **GroveClan:** the oldest Clan. Steady woodland hunters who remember everything and say so.

**Clan life.** Kits live in the nursery. At six moons a kit becomes an apprentice with a
mentor, and an apprentice becomes a warrior when the leader decides she's ready. Leaders lead,
medicine cats heal, and elders tell the old stories. Patrols mark the borders, hunters fill the
prey pile, and apprentices train in the Training Hollow. Every full moon the Clans meet under
truce on Gathering Island. Above it all runs the Sky River (the Milky Way), where the cats who
came before watch over the Clans and sometimes send dreams.

**Cats from outside.** A cat born outside the Clans can join, if she earns the Clan's trust.
Every player's cat was born a pillow cat and joins CrystalClan, so the Clan doubts her at first.

**Names.** Each player names their own cat. Clan-born kits are named for how they look or act
(Minnowkit); a cat who joins from outside chooses a Clan name when she joins. An apprentice's
name ends in -paw (Minnowpaw). A warrior's second half is earned by what she does (Minnowsong).
A leader's name ends in -star.

## Otters: the Holt

The otters run the Crossing, so they hear all the news. They're playful, nosy and chatty,
they juggle pebbles, and every otter keeps a favorite stone and gives one to each new friend.
At full moon they raft the Clans upriver to Gathering Island.

## Dragons: the Mistscales

The Mistscales live in the mist of the gorge at the Roaring. Grown dragons are as big as a
barn and almost never seen; dragonets are about the size of a heron. They love smooth stones
and shiny things, and they eat a lot of fish. Their greatest thanks is a **mist stone**: grey
until moonlight hits it, then flashing blue and green, given only to someone who saved one of
their own. Other tribes (the Tidescales of the Bay, the Ridgescales of the mountains) are
stories for later.

## The bad guys

Foxes, weasels, snakes, dogs and badgers are more likely bad than good, so the Clans are wary
of them. But not every one is bad, and each is judged by what it does. (Coil the snake trades
secrets and helps, a little.)

- **Foxes:** charming, smiling liars who would rather steal than hunt.
- **Weasels:** quick, slinky gangs who sneak and grab, often for a fox.
- **Snakes:** they hiss secrets and trade them. Never trust one all the way.
- **Dogs:** Tallwalker dogs, loud, bouncy and not very clever. The danger is being chased.
- **Badgers:** huge, grumpy diggers from upriver. Rare, and the Clans' scariest stories.

## Tone

- **Not dark.** Nobody dies, and the worst injury is a hurt wing. Fights are training with
  claws sheathed, or chases. Bad guys get outwitted, exposed and chased off. Scary means a dark
  place, always with a friend nearby.
- **Friendships, no romance.**
- **Any humor goes**, hairballs and burps included.
- **Lissa reads it herself:** short screens, dialogue first, words a Warriors reader knows.
- **Every chapter ends warm** (proposed). Tension lives in the middle of a chapter. Each one
  ends in her nest with the dream question, and the hook to the next is curious, not scary
  ("something on a tower roof roared back"), so a session never ends on a fright.

## Themes

**Aron's four (2026-10-03)**
1. Mystery and adventure.
2. Warmth, coziness and comfort.
3. Reassurance, bravery, friendship and teamwork.
4. Humor: funny or unexpected situations, and the occasional zany character.

**Six more the story already carries (proposed)**
5. **Getting better with practice.** Nobody is born knowing the Counts, or how to fly. Mistakes
   are how you learn.
6. **Noticing, and thinking for yourself.** Look closely, ask questions, and check what everyone
   "knows." Being young doesn't make you wrong.
7. **Fairness.** Judge others by what they do, not by how they look. Everyone deserves a fair
   hearing. She was doubted for being a pillow cat, so she gives Drizzle the fair hearing she got.
8. **Kindness to the small and the hurt.**
9. **Belonging and home.** The Clan is family. Goodbyes can be happy and sad at once.
10. **Wonder in familiar places.** Her own river, seen the way a cat sees it.

Bravery here is mostly the inside kind: stepping out the door, sitting through the Roar, facing
her worry, telling the truth, speaking up for someone who's being blamed. Fighting stays training.

## The Counts

In the Clans, the times tables are called **the Counts**: a warrior skill as important as the
hunter's crouch. A cat who knows her Counts can feed her whole Clan, and she can always tell
when something is missing. Each Count has a picture every cat carries:

| Count | Picture |
|---|---|
| 1s | tails: one per cat |
| 2s | ears: two per cat |
| 3s | a patrol: three cats |
| 4s | paws: four per cat |
| 5s | claws on one forepaw |
| 10s | claws on both forepaws |

Teaching order: 1, 2, 5, 10, then 3 and 4. How module one teaches them is in
[`module-1.md`](module-1.md#tallyheart-and-the-counts).

## The series

1. **Module one, solo:** *The Dragon Under the Bridge.* Each friend plays it alone, hunts
   for the seven scattered dragonets, and ends it still an apprentice, holding a mist stone.
2. **The group game, later:** the friends meet at the Crossing and compare which dragonets
   each of them found. Finished players leave their favorite stones on a cairn at the
   Crossing, which is how they first learn the others exist.
3. **Warrior names come in a later module**, and what each player did in module one is part
   of theirs.
4. **A hook:** a badger's track in the mud upriver.
