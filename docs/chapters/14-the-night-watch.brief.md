## What the chapter is

**Two frosty days on, the store holds two hundred forty-five, and she counts it every morning. After a lesson in the
Hollow (the same trick in three digits; Chispapaw says «¡Desagrupo!» first), a patrol to the wall's end finds holes in
ReedClan's bank with two long lines and three little pokes beside them, a young tom wearing a badger-proof pebble, and
Sloshbank boasting that ReedClan's store beats theirs by two hundred twenty-eight ("A store by the river smells like
supper. If I were a badger…"). Glintstar sets a watch on the store, and the first is hers: her, Chispapaw and the grey
tom, who is asleep in a heartbeat. When the moon comes up, Tallyheart pads by and tells how she got her torn ear; they
count the tricky ones to keep awake. Then, from behind the store: snuffle, snuffle, and a sneeze so big the stones hum.
"Bless you," says the grey tom, fast asleep. "Thank you," says a grumpy voice. At first light every piece is there, and
big prints, five toes in a row, lead downriver, toward the Holt.**

Module 2's first night: wonder (the store by moonlight, silver, never dark), a mentor's steadiness (she keeps the watch;
nobody goes looking), comedy (a sleeping guard who blesses a sneeze, a badger with manners), and a story Tallyheart has
never told. The badger is a sneeze, a voice and some prints: heard, never seen, and funny before it is anything else.
